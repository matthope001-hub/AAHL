DIVISION WINNER PICKS

At the start of the season, each entry picks one team to win each of the 4 NHL divisions (Atlantic, Metropolitan, Central, Pacific). At the end of the regular season, each correct division winner pick earns +5 bonus points. Maximum of 20 bonus points from division picks.

INJURY RESERVE (IR)

Players placed on official NHL IR by their team are flagged in the pool. IR players continue to accumulate points when they return to play — their stats never stop counting. IR is informational only and does not change scoring. Commissioner manages IR flags manually.
